01 / PROFILE

About Me.

Research experience, academic foundations, and hands-on robotics engineering — one connected journey.

NGHE-NHAN TRUONG · AI RESEARCH ENGINEER

I believe every breakthrough begins with imagination, and the most intuitive way to imagine is through visual representations. That spark is what drove me into Computer Vision and its transformative real-world applications.

Currently, I serve as an AI Research Engineer – Professional Manager (PM) at Yuil Robotics, where I shape the vision technical roadmap and specialize in adapting and deploying Foundation Models on NVIDIA edge devices for real-time robotic perception.

I completed my Master’s degree in Computer Science at Sejong University and my Bachelor’s in Engineering at HCMUTE. Driven by a deep curiosity for how machines perceive and interact with three-dimensional space, I am actively seeking prospective Ph.D. opportunities in 3D Computer Vision and Physical AI.

Outside the lab, my life philosophy is simple: Learning is a lifelong journey, and the happiness is in the journey, not the destination.
